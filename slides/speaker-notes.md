# Cargo office hours, 29 October 2026

## Slide 1: opening

Keep this slide up as people arrive. Spend about two minutes framing the demo.

“Let's give Cargo five accounts and our targeting rules, ask which ones deserve attention, then correct one decision and see what changes.”

Move to the terminal. Show the short targeting brief, the prepared accounts and the play. Inspect the priorities and reasons in Cargo. Return to the terminal to give feedback, inspect the proposed correction and approve it before rerunning.

## Slide 2: closing

Return after showing the changed result. Spend the remaining time on questions.

“Which decision would you start with?”

Explain that the evidence can come from calls, buying signals or account research. The same-sample rerun demonstrates changed behaviour; validation on fresh accounts is a next step. An approved change can update context, data or code depending on the cause of the mistake.

## Slide 3: try it yourself

Leave the prompt visible for a screenshot. Participants open a new folder in Claude Code, Cursor or Codex and paste the prompt. They supply five accounts and answer the targeting questions. The linked installation guide covers Cargo sign-in and skills. Conductor and the presenter's custom demo commands are not required.

This recreates the workflow's idea. After the first correction and comparison, encourage testing on fresh accounts.

## Presentation controls

- Open office-hours.html in Chrome. It is self-contained and works offline.
- Arrow keys or Space: advance. Left arrow: go back. Home/End: first/last slide.
- F: fullscreen. N: private presenter-note overlay; hide before sharing the screen.
- Move the mouse for controls. Controls fade after 1.6 seconds.
- office-hours.pdf contains all three slides as a backup. No notes or controls are printed.
