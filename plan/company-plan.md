## The one goal

Make all **10 fixture decisions** reproducible by **2026-10-29**: five baseline priorities and five priorities after the reviewed services exclusion. The unfilled Manifest scaffold started with zero demo account cases when inspected on 2026-10-29.

This is a synthetic teaching example owned by Tariq Minhas. It carries no revenue target, customer outcome or claim that the targeting rule works beyond the supplied sample.

## The forecast

The forecast here is verification coverage, not revenue.

| Source | Contribution | Assumption |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Baseline rules | Five account-priority checks | Prepared evidence and the evaluation date remain fixed. |
| Reviewed services exclusion | Five account-priority checks | Only the rule version and services exclusion change. |

## The three moves

1. Make the brief and its limits readable in [the demo ICP](../context/icp/office-hours-demo.md) and [scoring motion](../context/motion/office-hours.md).
2. Declare one manual account-prioritisation play in [infra/office-hours/](../infra/office-hours/), with a reason and next step for every account.
3. Use [the office-hours initiative](../initiatives/2026-10-29-office-hours-demo.md) and [regression checks](../evals/office-hours/) to review the services exclusion and compare the same five accounts.

The scope is one decision loop. Live enrichment, customer outreach, CRM updates and unattended execution are outside this demo.
