## Firmographic

- B2B accounts with 50 to 200 employees.
- Industry and geography are unspecified in this teaching example.
- The sample evaluates buying-signal freshness as of 2026-10-29.

## Pains

The hypothetical product helps sales teams manage their operations. This is a teaching premise, not validated market research or Cargo's own customer profile.

## Triggers

Prioritise an otherwise suitable account when the supplied evidence contains a sales-team expansion signal within 45 days of the evaluation date. A missing or unsupported signal does not justify first priority.

## Disqualifiers

The baseline excludes non-B2B accounts and accounts outside the size range. It deliberately has no business-model exclusion. The session's human feedback adds services businesses to that exclusion list after review.

## How to identify

Use the five synthetic records in `evals/office-hours/accounts.json`. Each record contains prepared evidence and a `fixture:` source reference. Missing targeting facts require research. The active machine-readable rules live in `context/motion/office-hours/rules.json`.
